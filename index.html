About the Virus
Transmission
Is it contagious?
Rarely person-to-person
The Andes strain is the only hantavirus that can spread between people — but only through prolonged, very close contact like sharing a cabin. It does not spread through the air like flu.
Treatment
Is there a vaccine?
No vaccine, no cure
No approved vaccine or antiviral exists. Treatment is supportive care only — oxygen, ICU monitoring, and in severe cases ECMO, a machine that takes over heart and lung function.
Severity
How deadly is it?
35–50% fatality rate
Starts as fever, fatigue and muscle ache. Within days it can escalate to severe pneumonia, respiratory failure and shock. Even with ICU care, the Andes strain kills 35–50% of those infected.
Origin
How do you get it?
Infected rodent contact
Spread through inhaling particles from infected rodent urine or droppings. The MV Hondius cases likely contracted it during a birdwatching trip in Argentina, home to the long-tailed pygmy rice rat.
